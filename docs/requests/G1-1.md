# G1-1: the seam test pins pre-G1 answers for five unseen phrasings

From: goal G1 (understanding round 1), branch `understanding-r1`.
For: the owner of `tests/test_language_seam.py` (S2-min / W1). G1 did not edit it.

## What fails

`tests/test_language_seam.py::test_output_is_byte_identical_to_the_dialogue_before_the_seam`
compares every turn with `EXPECTED`, recorded on 4adc504 before `realize` was wired
in. Its cases come from `data/benchmarks/unseen_phrasing_v1.json`. G1 exists to turn
holds on unseen phrasings into answers, so five cases now differ. None of the
changed turns is a wrong answer:

| Case | Turn | 4adc504 (EXPECTED) | G1 HEAD |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ko-01 | 2 (count question) | over-bound hold | 3개입니다. |
| ko-02 | 1 (possession statement) | no reply | recorded, 7 |
| ko-02 | 2 (count question) | no reply | 7개입니다. |
| ko-04 | 2 (count question) | no reply | 3개입니다. |
| ko-08 | 3 (count question) | no reply | hold: an earlier transfer to that person is unread |
| ko-10 | 2 (count question) | no reply | 2개입니다. |

The other 15 cases and every other seam test pass.

## What the test means to guard

Its docstring: "The stub must return exactly what the dialogue returned before
the seam." That is a property of `realize`, not of the engine's understanding.
Frozen strings also pin the engine, so every understanding round will break it.

## Options (the owner decides)

1. Re-record `EXPECTED` for the five cases at the G1 commit. Simple; the next round
   breaks it again.
2. Compare, per case, the answers of the same run with `reasoning_context.realize`
   patched to identity against the answers with the real `realize`. This keeps the
   guard ("realize changes nothing yet") and stops pinning understanding.
