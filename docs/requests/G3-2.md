# G3-2: a count of one is said in the plural, or held, for irregular nouns

From: goal G3 (understanding round 3), branch `understanding-r3`.
For: the owner of `marco/language/realizer/` (goal W2).

## What G3 declared

`styles/english.json` `명사수.irregular` (the G3.1 rules commit): the closed classes of
English irregular plurals (vowel mutation, -en, person/people, zero plurals,
f/fe -> ves, consonant + o -> oes). The parser keys "one knife" and "how many
knives" on one fact (`relational_semantics.declared_plural`).

## What the realizer says

| Statement | Question | Engine | Realized reply |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Haru has one knife. | How many knives does Haru have? | answered, fact `[Haru knives, count, 1]` | `1 knives.` |
| Ilse has one goose. | How many geese does Ilse have? | answered, fact `[Ilse geese, count, 1]` | `This answer is on hold.` |
| Ilse has one child. | How many children does Ilse have? | answered, fact `[Ilse children, count, 1]` | `This answer is on hold.` |

The second and third are answered by the engine (status `answered`) but the
reply holds, so the gate scores them `unverifiable` (no quantity in the answer).

## Request

Say the singular for a count of one from the same declaration
(`PackModel.language` carries `noun_number`, including `irregular`): the
singular of a declared irregular plural is its key (`geese` -> `goose`). A
reply that holds should not leave the turn's status `answered` (see G3-3).
